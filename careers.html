Abstract Artists

Traditional artists with flexible art styles, with an intuition for design under constraints and an affinity for abstract expression. Due to the need to maintain a level of accuracy and to remain true to personality snapshots, the candidate must demonstrate consistency in their understanding and execution of colour theory, composition, visual language and craftsmanship. This is an independent contractor position, with candidates retaining 100% of their work's intellectual property with disclaimer that Sitôt's snapshot results were used, as well as 100% of artist fees received for each delivered art piece. Client lists can be acquired independently or provided by the studio on a per-need basis.
