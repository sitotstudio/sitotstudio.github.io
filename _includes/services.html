We manually write a short description of each result email we send, so please allow 24 hours to receive yours!
Sitôt's snapshot questionnaire captures who you are at a moment in time, since, like our physical body, our thoughts are also expected to change and grow with time.

The snapshot questionnaire consists of 60 questions we wrote that build an understanding of you; how you perceive yourself, how you portray yourself to others, how others perceive you, how you want to be perceived, how much your environment influences you, and additional questions derived from CPAI traits. Your responses create a bar chart that describes your personality in visual language, forming your visual descriptor profile.

The artworks produced at Sitôt are all individually hand-painted using professional gallery-grade archival acrylics, on 100% cotton canvases, hand-stretched over frames made of locally-sourced wood from the pacific northwest.

Our canvas sizes start at 10"x10", at 450CAD. There is a limit of 3-5 bookings per month for our smallest sizes with an estimated 2-3 weeks production time to account for design time and paint layers curing properly, so only a completed payment ensures your spot is claimed for the month. Work begins once we receive both your Snapshot results and your booking, and you will receive a confirmation email from us acknowledging your order.
For larger custom sizes, please send us a quote request at info@sitot.ca and we will be in touch with you to ensure your artwork fits your space and timelines.

Sitôt is a fine arts, cognitive sciences, and software research studio that creates custom abstract art pieces that accurately captures the look and feel of your personality, visualizing who you are on canvas.
Each personality portrait's design is 100% human-created, and is a result of your answers to the snapshot questionnaire, cross-modal perception theories applied through cutting-edge software, and the irreplaceable eye & craftsmanship of a human artist.
