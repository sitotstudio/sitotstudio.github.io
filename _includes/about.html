Sitôt Arts & Tech Residency Program

Applications begin in 2027
Learn how to paint personality portraits, strategies to integrate tech with art that respects human creativity, and exhibit your work!

  • Week 1:

    Designing from snapshot scores

    Learn how each visual descriptor translates to paint strokes, and acquire the understanding needed to start designing your own portrait.

  • Week 2:

    Exploitative vs supportive tech

    How can we merge tech and art while respecting creativity? What differentiates tech that exploits and tech that nourishes humanity?

  • Week 3:

    Understanding software development

    The software world needs the perspective of traditional artists. This starts with making coding no longer intimidating for creatives, with insights into the tech industry from Microsoft alum.

  • Week 4:

    Portrait presentations

    Show and tell of your personality portrait made during the residency. Peer-support regarding portrait design, balancing visual descriptor results, or technical execution difficulties.

  • Exhibition:

    Invite friends & family, it's a gallery party!
