Snapshot Questionnaire

Before we begin, please enter your unique ID (uid) if you were provided one, this allows us to link your responses to your order. A new UID will be assigned to you otherwise.
We will send your results in bar chart form to your e-mail once we run your responses through our system!

During the questionnaire, be as specific as you'd like. Answers range from 0.0 (0%) (hard disagree) to 1.00 (100%) (fully agreed), adjustable in increments of 0.01 (1%).
If you're unsure or feel something doesn't apply to you, choose 0.5 (50%). Keep in mind there are no wrong answers!
